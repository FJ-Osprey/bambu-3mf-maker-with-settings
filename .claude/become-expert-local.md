# /become-expert: local framing for this repo

- **Domain**: design for additive manufacturing (DfAM) with FDM printers: functional parts and multi-part
  assemblies for a hobbyist builder, PLA and PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, a Bambu A1 mini.
- **Authorities**: manufacturer design guides (Bambu Lab wiki, Prusa Knowledge Base, Hubs, Protolabs, Xometry,
  Formlabs), material data sheets (Bambu, Prusament), plastics makers' snap-fit manuals (Bayer/Covestro, BASF), tested
  data (CNC Kitchen), design-for-assembly (Boothroyd-Dewhurst, poka-yoke), DfAM literature. The source lists at the
  foot of each `docs/design-for-print/*.md` file are the working bibliography.
- **Apply target**: `docs/DESIGN-FOR-PRINT.md` and its topic files; any print design the session is working on (the
  calling project's own print folder and design notes, outside this repo).
- **Register target**: persona in `personas/<slug>_persona.md`, research cache in `personas/<slug>_research_cache.md`,
  both listed in this repo's `CLAUDE.md` read-first list. This repo is public: generic wording, no project names,
  people or personal details.
- **Gate that still applies**: section 0 of `docs/DESIGN-FOR-PRINT.md` (design for the builder) and the "how to hold
  two parts together" order. A persona is a lens, not a licence to skip them.
