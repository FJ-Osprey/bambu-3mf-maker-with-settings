# 9. Tools and consumables worth owning for functional prints

Back to the [entry point](../DESIGN-FOR-PRINT.md). Section 0 says: ask the builder what they have before designing
around anything, and recommend freely. This is the list to recommend from. Prices are typical US prices seen in 2026
on store listings (several from search extracts, not checked one by one); treat them as approximate.

| Item | What it enables or replaces | Typical price | Recommended by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Digital calipers, 150 mm | Every coupon and compensation measurement; sizing bought parts before designing round them | $28-48 (Mitutoyo about $158) | Most maker tool lists (thatpainter: "the highest-value upgrade for functional prints"; siraya; filamentfinders) |
| Deburring tool (swivel blade) | Takes off the elephant's-foot lip, brim edges and hole burrs, so fits work without sanding | about $10 | filamentfinders; thatpainter; siraya; Prusa's post-processing kit |
| Flush cutters | Clean support and brim removal | about $10 | all four lists |
| Needle files, sanding sticks | Final fit-up of a hole or a mating face | about $10 (10-piece diamond set) | Prusa kit ("adjusting clearance holes and smoothing mating surfaces"); thatpainter; siraya |
| Soldering iron with insert tips | Heat-set threaded inserts: strong, reusable threads in a printed hole (section 3) | Pinecil V2 $26-36; insert tip set about $16 | CNC Kitchen (the test data) |
| Heat-set insert assortment, M2 / M2.5 / M3 | The threads themselves | $12-30 for a few hundred | CNC Kitchen; ruthex |
| Screw assortment: M2/M3 socket head, and self-tapping screws for plastic | Screwing into printed pilots and inserts; a known screw makes the pilot ladder meaningful | $9-23 each | engineerdog (thread-forming screws for plastic) |
| Neodymium disc magnets, 6 × 2 and 6 × 3 mm | Tool-free lids and locating (section 5) | $10 for a few hundred | magnet vendors; draftid |
| CA glue (medium) and activator | Fast bonds on close-fitting joints, magnets in pockets | about $10 | magnetenspecialist; Prusa glue test |
| 2-part epoxy (5-minute) | Gap-filling and larger glued faces | about $10 | Prusa glue test |
| PETG filament | Tougher parts for snaps, clips and anything warm (section 2) | Bambu PETG HF about $20-23 a kg | Bambu |
| Hand reamer set | Opening a printed hole a few tenths to an exact size, when it truly must be exact (section 1) | $10-35 | Rahix; Syntax3D Lab (reaming, not drilling) |

Not recommended for functional PLA/PETG by any source read: a tap set (inserts do the job better), feeler gauges.

**A short shopping list** for a builder starting functional prints who has a printer and calipers: deburring tool,
flush cutters, a heat-set insert kit with a matching soldering-iron tip, an M2/M3 screw kit, a magnet assortment.
About $50-70 together.
