# Changelog

## v0.9.1 (2026-10-31)
Part 05 replaced. The old eye plug stood proud of the sphere and wanted glue. The new one has the bracket eye's curved seat, so it stops flush, and
squash ribs that grip without glue. Three tightnesses, marked with 1, 2 or 3 notches; 3 fit best on the printer it was tried on.

## v0.9 (2026-10-31)
First public set. Front half now comes in two versions: a lay-in cable notch open to the rim (new, not printed yet) and the closed slot
(printed). Cables also lay in at the back arch, over the riser's rim.
